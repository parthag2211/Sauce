---
name: Hiring release boundary
description: Why the first hiring release stays internal-test-only without automatic scoring or live transcription.
---

Keep the first hiring release internal-test-only until provider consent/retention decisions and scoring anchors/weights are approved. Sagar owns the scoring approval.

**Why:** The handoff explicitly withholds approval for real applicant collection, paid provider calls and invented numerical ratings. An unconfigured score or unavailable transcript must not silently become a default or synthetic result.

**How to apply:** Extend the live provider only after the approval boundary is resolved; preserve original versus reviewed transcripts and never interpret accepted as hired. Do not market the current manual-review path as functioning automatic ASR.
